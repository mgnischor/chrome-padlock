Padlock creates strong, random passwords and passphrases on your device. Copy the result with a click, or insert it straight into a text field.

How it works
- Every character or word is drawn with your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator, and the strength meter shows the exact entropy in bits.
- Choose the length (4 to 128 characters) and the character types: lowercase, uppercase, digits and symbols. You can avoid look-alike characters such as 0, O, 1, l and I, and exclude any characters you do not want.
- Passphrases: switch to passphrase mode for 3 to 12 random words, such as abruptly-frosting-gamble-wiry-snowman-cabbage. Choose the separator, capital letters and an optional digit. The words come from a built-in list of 7,776 English words.
- Copy the result, or right-click a text field and choose “Insert secure password”. You can also press Ctrl+Shift+U (Command+Shift+U on a Mac) to insert one into the field you are typing in.
- Strength test: type or paste a password to see its estimated entropy in bits and how long it would take to crack. It is analyzed on your device and is never stored or sent.
- Optional: clear the clipboard automatically 30 seconds to 2 minutes after copying.
- Optional: keep a list of the last 50 generated passwords. It is off by default, held unencrypted in browser memory only, and erased when Chrome closes.
- Light and dark themes, and 30 interface languages.

Privacy
Padlock runs entirely on your device. It collects no data, makes no network requests and contains no ads or analytics. It is not a password manager: it never saves or syncs what it generates.

Permissions
Padlock accesses a page only when you use its right-click menu entry or its keyboard shortcut, and only the tab and field you used. The clipboard permissions are optional and requested only if you turn on automatic clipboard clearing.
